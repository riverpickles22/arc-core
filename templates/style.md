# <Story Title> — Prose Style Guide

> Governs the manuscript (`prose/**`). Directional docs (`vision.md`) say what
> the book is; this file says how its sentences behave. Canon YAML still wins
> on any question of fact (conventions §1).
>
> Rules here are written to be checkable — anyone drafting a scene, human or
> machine, runs the checklist at the bottom before showing prose to the author.
> This guide is descriptive of the author's voice: it grows by extraction from
> the author's reactions to drafted prose, not by invention (conventions §10).
