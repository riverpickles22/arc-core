## The watch

A first-order light burned paraffin under a clockwork-rotated lens. The keeper's
night was structured around the mechanism rather than the weather: trim the wick,
wind the drive weight, and log the hour [@example-keeper-manual]. The routine's
rigidity is the useful fact for fiction — it gives a solitary character a day
with a spine, and it makes any deviation legible to the reader.

## Isolation

Remote stations were provisioned by boat on a monthly cycle, weather permitting,
which in winter often meant not permitting [@example-keeper-manual]. Extended
solitude was an occupational condition, not an anomaly.

## What this story takes, and what it changes

- **Takes:** the shape of the watch, the paraffin-and-brass texture, the supply
  cadence.
- **Changes:** Whitcombe Light is invented (`real: false`), and a single keeper
  works the station where a real service of the period generally posted two or
  more. This is a deliberate divergence in service of the solitude premise —
  recorded here rather than hidden.
