# The Whitcombe light log

> Canonical data: `canon/entities/objects/keepers-log.yaml` — YAML wins on conflict.

> **Proposed, not canon.** The author has not decided whether the log is the
> board's record or Ines's own habit. Reference it; do not rest a scene on it.

A bound book kept on the lamp-room sill beside the shears. Six ruled columns —
date, hour, wind, glass, sea, remarks — filled at every watch. Ines has never
left a column empty, and writes *nothing* out in full on the nights when
nothing is what happened, because a dash could have been anyone's.

## What is at stake in ratifying it

If the log is official, the night of the wreck has a document in it. Ines
writes the hour and the weather while the Aurelia is still afloat, and what
she puts in *remarks* becomes the only contemporaneous account of a night
nineteen people were on the water for. A board reads it afterward.

If it is only hers, that page is nobody's business, and the same six words
mean something else entirely — a private record of a night she did not know
was a night yet.
