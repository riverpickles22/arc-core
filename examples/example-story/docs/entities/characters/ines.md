# Ines Vargas

> Canonical data: `canon/entities/characters/ines.yaml` — YAML wins on conflict.

## Overview
Keeper of [[place.whitcombe-light|Whitcombe Light]] since 1908. The novel's
question is not whether her solitude is bad for her — it isn't — but what it
costs her when nineteen strangers arrive uninvited and she can only keep nine.

## Journey by era
**The keeping years.** Sufficiency. The watch, the log, the dog. Nothing to
narrate but the texture of a life that works.

**After the wreck.** [[event.the-wreck]] puts people in her kitchen for six
days. When they leave, the quiet she chose is still there and no longer fits.
