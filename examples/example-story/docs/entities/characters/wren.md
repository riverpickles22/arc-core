# Wren

> Canonical data: `canon/entities/characters/wren.yaml` — YAML wins on conflict.

## Overview
The harbor dog who followed a supply boat up the point in 1908 and stayed. He
is the story's second protagonist without ever being given a thought the prose
has to invent for him.

## Journey by era
**The keeping years.** Total predictability, which suits him.

**After the wreck.** Into the surf four times during [[event.the-wreck]], out
three times carrying someone. Afterward, lame — and will not cross the tide
line again.
