# Whitcombe Light

> Canonical data: `canon/entities/places/whitcombe-light.yaml` — YAML wins on conflict.

## Overview
A first-order light and keeper's cottage on a basalt point, four miles of cart
track from the nearest village. Invented (`real: false`), composited from
northern-coast stations of the period — see
`research/topics/lighthouse-keeping.md`.

## The place itself
Wind that never fully stops, so its stopping is the alarming thing. Paraffin and
brass above, damp wool and peat smoke below. Ninety-one stairs; the eighty-fourth
is loose, and stays loose for the whole book.

## Its people
[[char.ines]] keeps it ([[rel.ines-light]]); [[char.wren]] lives in it. Nobody
else does, until [[event.the-wreck]].
